rclone ctx

rclone ctx is a personal file-sync setup. It uses the open-source tool rclone so that its owner can keep his own project files in his own Google Drive and move them between his own computers. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Privacy policy

rclone ctx accesses only the Google Drive of the account that signs in to it, and only to copy that person's own files to and from their own computers. Files are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload.

No data is collected, stored, shared with or sold to anyone else. The app has no server, no analytics and no advertising. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact: the account owner, via the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
